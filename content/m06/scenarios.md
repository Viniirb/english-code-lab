# Three bugs

- **Bug 1**: The login button does nothing on mobile. On a phone, a user taps the button and the page stays the same. On a desktop it works.
- **Bug 2**: The total price is wrong when the discount is 100 percent. The cart shows a price below zero instead of zero.
- **Bug 3**: The app crashes when the user uploads a photo larger than 5 MB. The screen goes white and a message appears in the console.
