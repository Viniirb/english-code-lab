# Module 11: Async updates (stand-up in writing)

Remote teams write short daily updates. Write 5 updates in `work/m11/updates.md` (`## Day 1` ... `## Day 5`),
each with three lines (see `content/m11/TEMPLATE.md`):

- `Yesterday:` what you finished, in the **past tense** (fixed, wrote, merged, finished).
- `Today:` your plan, with **will** or **going to**.
- `Blockers:` `None.` or the problem in 6+ words and what help you need.

Each update has 20 to 90 words. Use real things from your week.
