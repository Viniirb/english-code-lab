# Quiz: reading review comments

What does the reviewer mean? Answer in `work/m08/answers.txt` like `q1: b`.

**q1.** "nit: maybe rename `d` to `date`."  What does "nit" mean?

- a) A serious bug.
- b) A small suggestion that is not important.
- c) A security problem.

**q2.** "LGTM, thanks!"  What does it mean?

- a) Looks good to me.
- b) Let me test more.
- c) Last git merge today.

**q3.** "Could we add a test for the empty list?"

- a) An angry order.
- b) A complaint about the speed.
- c) A polite request.

**q4.** "This is blocking: it breaks the login."

- a) You must fix it before the merge.
- b) It is optional.
- c) It is already fixed.

**q5.** "I am not sure about this part. What do you think?"

- a) The reviewer approves the code.
- b) The reviewer asks for your opinion.
- c) The reviewer rejects the whole change.

**q6.** "Please rebase on main."

- a) Delete your branch.
- b) Rename your branch.
- c) Update your branch with the latest changes from main.
