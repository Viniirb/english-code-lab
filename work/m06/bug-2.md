# Total price is below zero when the discount is 100 percent

## Steps to reproduce

1. Add one product to the cart.
2. Apply the coupon with 100 percent discount.
3. Open the cart page and read the total.

## Expected behavior

The total price should be zero.

## Actual behavior

The cart shows a negative price, for example minus 0.01.

## Environment

Desktop, Chrome 126, app version 2.4.1.
