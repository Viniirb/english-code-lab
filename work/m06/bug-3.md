# App crashes when a photo is larger than 5 MB

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the profile page.
2. Choose a photo that is larger than 5 MB.
3. Press the upload button.

## Expected behavior

The app should show a clear message that the photo is too large.

## Actual behavior

The screen goes white and an error appears in the console.

## Environment

Android 14, Chrome 126, app version 2.4.1.
